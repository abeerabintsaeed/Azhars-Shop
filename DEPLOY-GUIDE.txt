AZHARS - PUTTING THE STORE ONLINE (GitHub + Railway)
====================================================

What you will end up with: a real web address (like https://azhars-store.up.railway.app)
that works in any browser AND can be installed like an app on Windows, Mac, Android and iPhone.

Two free-to-open accounts are needed: GitHub (stores your files) and Railway (runs the store).
Railway is not free after its trial (see PART 0). Plan for about 60-90 minutes the first time.


PART 0 - READ THIS FIRST
------------------------
* Railway: new accounts get a one-time trial credit (about $5, for up to 30 days). After that the
  "Hobby" plan is about $5 USD per month. Check the current price at railway.com/pricing.
* Your products, orders and customers live in a "Volume" (a permanent disk) that you attach in Part 3.
  WITHOUT the Volume everything is erased each time the store restarts. Do not skip it.
* The store does NOT send emails or SMS yet. You will not be told when an order arrives, so you
  must check Admin > Orders yourself (the installed app makes this easy).
* Card payments are not live yet. Online, only Cash on Delivery is switched on at first; you can
  switch on Bank / JazzCash / Easypaisa in Admin > Settings after typing your REAL account details.
* There are no Terms, Privacy or Returns pages yet.


PART 1 - GITHUB (about 15 minutes)
----------------------------------
1. Go to github.com and click Sign up. Create the account and confirm your email.
2. Top right, click the "+" icon, then "New repository".
3. Repository name: azhars-store     Choose PRIVATE.
   Do NOT tick "Add a README". Click "Create repository".
4. On the empty page, click the link "uploading an existing file".
5. On your computer: unzip azhars-store-online.zip. Open the folder called azhars.
   Press Ctrl+A (Cmd+A on Mac) to select EVERYTHING INSIDE it, then DRAG it all onto the GitHub page.
   (Drag the contents, NOT the azhars folder itself.)
6. Wait until every file finishes uploading. Scroll down, click "Commit changes".
7. CHECK: the repository's front page must show server.js and package.json directly in the list,
   plus a "public" folder. If you only see one folder called "azhars", you dragged the wrong thing:
   delete the repository (Settings, bottom) and redo steps 2-6.


PART 2 - RAILWAY: CREATE THE STORE (about 15 minutes)
-----------------------------------------------------
1. Go to railway.com and click Login. Choose "Login with GitHub" and allow it.
2. Click "New Project", then "Deploy from GitHub repo".
3. If asked, let Railway access GitHub. Choose "Only select repositories" and pick azhars-store.
4. Railway starts building. It will probably show "Crashed" or "Failed" after a minute.
   THIS IS EXPECTED - it is waiting for the settings you add next.
5. Click the azhars-store box, then the "Variables" tab. Add these (click "New Variable" for each):

     ADMIN_EMAIL           your email address (this is your admin login)
     ADMIN_PASSWORD        a long password, at least 10 characters. Write it down somewhere safe.
     RAILPACK_NODE_VERSION 24

   Every time you add variables Railway rebuilds. That is normal.


PART 3 - RAILWAY: PERMANENT STORAGE (5 minutes) - DO NOT SKIP
--------------------------------------------------------------
1. Go back to the project view (the canvas showing your azhars-store box).
2. Right-click an empty part of the canvas and choose "Volume"
   (or press Ctrl+K / Cmd+K, type "volume", and choose it).
3. When asked which service, choose azhars-store.
4. For the mount path type exactly:   /data
5. Wait for it to redeploy.


PART 4 - RAILWAY: GET YOUR WEB ADDRESS (3 minutes)
--------------------------------------------------
1. Click the azhars-store box, then "Settings", then find "Networking".
2. Click "Generate Domain". If it asks which port, leave the suggested one or the default.
3. You get an address like https://azhars-store-production.up.railway.app.
4. In the "Deployments" tab wait for a green "Active"/"Deployed" status.
   Click "View logs" - you should see  "AZHARS store is running"  and  "Online mode".
5. Open your address in a browser. Your store is online.


PART 5 - FIRST LOGIN AND SET-UP (20 minutes)
--------------------------------------------
1. Open   YOUR-ADDRESS/#/admin   and log in with the ADMIN_EMAIL and ADMIN_PASSWORD from Part 2.
2. The Dashboard must NOT show a red "Your data is not being saved permanently" box.
   If it does, Part 3 was not done correctly.
3. Admin > Settings: fill in your real store name, contact email/phone, address, delivery charge.
   Under Payment methods, type your real account details, then tick the ones you want. Save.
4. Admin > Products > Add product: add a real product (photo, price, colours and sizes).
   Or use Bulk upload for many at once.
5. Test like a customer: open your address in a private/incognito window, add the product to the bag,
   place an order with Cash on Delivery. Then in Admin > Orders open it and set it to Cancelled.
6. Admin > Settings > Download backup: do this now, and again every week.


PART 6 - INSTALL IT AS AN APP
-----------------------------
Open your web address on the device, then:
  iPhone / iPad : use SAFARI. Tap the Share button (square with arrow), then "Add to Home Screen", then Add.
  Android       : use CHROME. Tap the three dots, then "Install app" (or "Add to Home screen").
  Windows / Mac : use CHROME or EDGE. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  Or on any device: scroll to the bottom of the store and tap "Install the app" for the same steps.
The app icon (a green circle with an A) appears with your other apps.
This is not an App Store / Play Store listing; customers install it straight from your web address.


CHANGING THINGS LATER
---------------------
* Change the store's design or code: in GitHub open your repository, click "Add file",
  "Upload files", drop the changed files, "Commit changes". Railway updates the store by itself.
  Your products, orders and customers are safe on the Volume.
* Change your own admin password: sign in, click the person icon, Password.
* Forgot the admin password: in Railway Variables set ADMIN_PASSWORD to a new one AND add
  ADMIN_RESET = true. After the store restarts, log in with the new password, then DELETE ADMIN_RESET
  (if you leave it, every restart puts that password back).
* Your own domain (like azhars.pk): Railway service > Settings > Networking > Custom Domain.
  You buy the domain from a registrar; Railway shows you the records to enter there.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
* Deployment shows a red "Crashed" after all variables are added:
  open the Deployments tab, click "View logs", read the last lines, and send them to me.
  The two common causes: ADMIN_EMAIL / ADMIN_PASSWORD missing (password needs 10+ characters), or
  the wrong Node version. For the second, check the variable RAILPACK_NODE_VERSION is set to 24.
* Red box on the Dashboard about data not saved: the Volume is missing or its path is not /data.
* Page loads but pictures are broken after an update: pictures live on the Volume; check it is still attached.
* Railway screens sometimes change names. If a button is not where this guide says, take a screenshot and
  send it to me.
